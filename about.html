The Process

How Pre-Ordering Works

The pre-order model is how we keep prices impossibly low. Here's exactly what happens from click to doorstep.

01
Browse Catalog

Explore our curated collection of 20+ international brands. All prices are shown in Naira — no conversion headaches.

02
Place Pre-Order

Add items to your cart, select your size, and pay securely. We batch orders every two weeks to maximize bulk savings.

03
Batch Sourcing

Our agents in the UK, US, Italy & Dubai purchase items from official brand stores with receipts — guaranteeing authenticity.

04
International Shipping

Items are shipped via our express logistics partner from the source country into Nigeria — tracked at every step.

05
Nigeria Delivery

Once cleared at customs, we dispatch your order for last-mile delivery to any state in Nigeria within 2–4 days.

06
You Receive & Verify

Your authentic item arrives with original packaging, tags, and receipt. Verify authenticity with the brand's official app if available.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know the items are actually authentic?
All items are purchased directly from the official brand store or an authorized retailer by our agents on the ground in UK, US, Italy, or Dubai. Every order comes with the original purchase receipt. For brands with authentication apps (like some Louis Vuitton products), we provide QR codes for self-verification. We've served 5,000+ customers without a single authenticity dispute.
Why is the delivery up to 30 days — isn't that long?
The 30-day window is what allows us to batch orders, source in bulk, and pass the massive savings on to you. Think of it like this: you save 50–70% compared to buying the same item locally. Most customers receive their orders in 18–25 days. The maximum wait is 30 days. If we ever exceed 30 days, you get a 15% discount on your next order — guaranteed.
Is my payment safe? What if I pay and get nothing?
Payments are processed through Paystack or Flutterwave — Nigeria's most trusted payment gateways. Your money is held securely until your order is dispatched. If for any reason we cannot fulfill your order, a full refund is processed within 48 hours. We've fulfilled 5,000+ orders with a 100% fulfillment rate since 2024.
What is the returns policy?
We offer full refunds if: (1) the item is not authentic, (2) the item arrives damaged, or (3) we send the wrong item. Due to the pre-order sourcing model, change-of-mind returns are not accepted. Please review your size guide carefully before ordering. We provide a sizing chart for every brand to help you choose correctly.
Do you deliver to all states in Nigeria?
Yes! We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT. Delivery fees are flat ₦3,500 nationwide. For Lagos and Abuja, delivery typically takes 1–2 days after the item arrives in Nigeria. For other states, allow 2–4 business days for last-mile delivery.
Can I request a brand or product that's not in your catalog?
Absolutely! Send us a WhatsApp message with the product details (name, size, color, the official retail price) and we'll give you a custom quote within 24 hours. Custom orders are subject to minimum pricing and may take slightly longer.
