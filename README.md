# LuxeNaija 👑

**LuxeNaija** is Nigeria's premier pre-order e-commerce platform for authentic international fashion luxury from global brands like **Gucci, Zara, Puma, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Balenciaga, Adidas, Versace, Burberry, and Fendi**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 👑 **100% Authentic Luxury**: Sourced directly from official brand stores in the UK, US, Italy, and Dubai.
- ✈️ **Pre-Order Sourcing Model**: High-end fashion delivered to your doorstep in Nigeria in **≤ 30 days**.
- 💳 **Nigerian Payment Gateways**: Built-in support for Paystack, Flutterwave, and Direct Bank Transfer.
- 📍 **Nationwide Delivery**: Seamless checkout with all 36 Nigerian states + FCT Abuja.
- 🔎 **Live Search & Catalog Filters**: Filter by brand, category (Bags, Sneakers, Clothing, Accessories), gender, and price range.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**: Designed with a high-end luxury dark aesthetic (gold accents, glassmorphism, smooth animations).

